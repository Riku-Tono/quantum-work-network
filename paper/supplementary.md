# Supplementary material

Companion to *Predictive Stopping and Memory-Assisted Adaptive Reconnection in Quantum Work Networks*. This supplement traces the manuscript claims to the milestone reports, recorded data and numerical audits. Values are in model units. Work values are signed net outputs unless identified as energy, gross work or input.

## Scope and source layout

The [public project record at revision `ca760bb`][project] describes the study through M30. The [M15B–M23 reproduction archive][m15b-m23] contains the first recovery studies. The [M24–M30 reproduction archive][m24-m30] contains the control studies. Paths starting with `Milestone_` identify the expanded working layout, with local links to the reports and tables. This structure allows the paper to travel with the working directories or alongside the public archives.

The physical continuation in the manuscript has zero drive and zero dephasing. In the preparation lineage, the M11 matched N=7 run used drive amplitude `0.18748395731510084`. The chain was dephased during preparation and the M18 continuation. S0 is the full density matrix immediately after the second M18 extraction, at absolute model time 15.90. Its SHA-256 is `4C9A5EE8172E505592C43A7B522476715D418CAFA0CC677C04B853E72CC6F9ED`. The M25–M28 clocks start at the recorded origin for each history. A04, B04 and C04 are fourth-extraction states of the three M23 repeated policies. Matched post-switch comparisons in M28–M30 retain the original clock and exclude work before the anchor. The six separately prepared U states use elapsed time from preparation.

## Rules and accounting details

**Connected stopping.** An observation of the terminal qubit and load is a 6 × 6 reduced density matrix. The implementation computes the load derivative analytically from the commutator, with eigenvalues of the load density matrix ordered along the corresponding energy levels. The unitary maps the descending eigenvectors to ascending energy eigenstates. Each eigenvector is phased so that its largest-magnitude component is real and nonnegative, with ties broken by the highest index. That choice fixes the interaction jump for the coupled extraction. The forecast then observes `D = J − dE_p/dt` on a 0.02 grid. The first valid forecast freezes the execution clock. At the selected time, the direct candidate work and the candidate work less the aligned-step discrepancy must each exceed `1e-12` in the RK4 branches. The sign-turn rule B is a separate policy. See [forecast rules](../Milestone_24/m24_rules.py), [connected observations](../Milestone_24/m24_physics.py) and [passivation implementation](../Milestone_23/m23_spectral.py).

**Mode switching.** The F2-R controller initializes in A. The baseline rate sums accepted net output at completed trials in `(0, 32]` and divides by 32. The rolling rate similarly uses `(t − 24, t]` and divides by 24. At trial boundaries from time 48, the rolling rate must be at most 45% of a positive baseline at two or more consecutive checks spanning at least 4 time units. The additional phase-assisted gate requires load ergotropy at most `1e-8` and `|c| ≥ 1e-5`, plus at least one of three signals:

1. The phase lies in `[−3π/4, −π/4]`, current is positive, and the ergotropy rate exceeds `1e-12`.
2. The circular phase change since the previous check is at least `π/3` and the previous `|c|` was at least `1e-5`.
3. The most recent accepted work is at most `1e-8` and its inter-extraction interval is at most 0.10.

F1 uses the slowdown trigger alone and chooses integer waits from 1 to 6 using completed trial work and time. In the matched-anchor comparison, RH applies this historical wait selector to the same anchor as the fixed and phase-input controllers. F2 used a strictly positive rate in the first clause. F2-R added the finite floor there. The C04 switch moved from a coarse/half disagreement of 70.38/68.90 to agreement at 70.38. The floor applies only to this clause. See [M27 controller](../Milestone_27/m27_control.py) and [M28 floor implementation](../Milestone_28/run_m28.py).

**Waiting conditions.** In M29–M30, let `c₀` be the coherence at the cut and `q = −Im(c)` the prospective-current signal at a waiting sample. P3 uses `q ≥ max(0.25|q₀|, 1e-11)`. P1 uses `q ≥ 0.25|c₀|` and `|c| ≥ 1e-5`. The input gate I compares `Re(c)` with `Re(c₀) + 1e-10`. The phase gate checks `arg(c) ∈ [−3π/4, −π/4]`, labeled A in the original reconnection tables and Aφ in the text. RP is P1 with both gates. Sampling uses waits of 1 to 6 in steps of 0.2, with the last eligible point forced if the conditions never pass. The remaining run time can truncate the grid or leave no wait. M30 compares all four gate combinations for each of P1 and P3. See the [M30 protocol](../Milestone_30/PROTOCOL_M30.md) and [information requirements](../Milestone_30/results/m30_information_design.csv).

**Work on a common clock.** For a trial with a wait, signed input is `−2g Re(c₀)` for disconnection and `+2g Re(cτ)` for reconnection. The work of coupled passivation uses the full Hamiltonian change and is zero when the candidate is skipped. M26's figure uses the completed-trial work series. M29 retains completion and executed-event definitions in its time tables. M30 uses the executed-event clock, booking a switch at its own time even if the trial is incomplete. At the M29 time-540 endpoint, the two definitions coincide. Compare intratrial values only where the clock and ledger definitions agree. See [M29 common-time tables](../Milestone_29/results/m29_time_comparison.csv), [M30 timeline](../Milestone_30/results/m30_timeline.csv) and [M30 work events](../Milestone_30/results/m30_work_events.csv).

## Stress state and numerical checks

In U05 trial six, the prospective current at the early reconnection was `+87.877e-6`, compared with `−215.339e-6` at the forced cap. The connected current formula gives those signs if the coupling is restored. The load exchange current during waiting is zero. The forced cap changed switch-on input from `+328.498e-6` to `−186.267e-6`. Each restoration had the same first-trial effect because either veto blocked the early choice. At later trials, branches reached different joint states. The interaction reported in the paper is the signed factorial contrast `W_IA − W_I − W_A + W_0` within the P3-based set. U05 is a studied failure mode that motivated the M30 design. In U06 an input-only veto could act independently, and delaying every apparent early opportunity could give up work. See [matched trial data](../Milestone_30/u05/trial6_summary.csv), [branch divergences](../Milestone_30/results/m30_policy_divergences.csv), [condition effects](../Milestone_30/results/m30_factorial.csv), and [endpoints](../Milestone_30/results/m30_endpoints.csv).

The group retention metric is the ratio of mean work to mean RP work. The protocol requires a group ratio of at least 95% and every state to reach at least 90% of its own RP reference. The criteria use an absolute shortfall tolerance of `1e-10` when RP is at or below that floor. In M29, P3 met both criteria on the F2-R and F1 anchor groups. Its six-state ratio was 78.75%. U06 accounted for about 76.6% of the RP work in the six-state group. In M30, P3+IA reached 107.59% group mean and 100.00% individual minimum on those six reused states. The exact propagation repeated decisions on the same coarse-prepared U05 and U06 initial densities. Component counts cover only the reconnection readout. See [M29 retention](../Milestone_29/results/m29_maintenance.csv), [M30 retention](../Milestone_30/results/m30_group_metrics.csv), and [M30 propagation provenance](../Milestone_30/results/m30_provenance.csv).
